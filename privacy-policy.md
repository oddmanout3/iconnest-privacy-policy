Privacy Policy for IconNest
Last updated: March 30, 2026

Thank you for using IconNest, developed by Preston Donovan. Your privacy is paramount.

### Core Policy: Zero Data Collection
IconNest does **not** collect, gather, transmit, or share any personal data, user content, photos, installed app information, or widget configurations. 

**IconNest does not request internet permission (`android.permission.INTERNET`)**, meaning the app has no network access and cannot send any data off your device under any circumstances.

---

### Local Device Storage & Features

Everything created or selected in IconNest stays exclusively on your device:

* **Custom Icons & Photos:** When you select photos or images from your device to create custom icons, they are cropped and rendered locally on your device. They are saved in your app's private local storage solely to display your widgets on your home screen.
* **Installed Applications & Icon Packs:** IconNest reads your list of installed apps and icon packs locally on your device only so you can choose which apps to place in your custom widgets and shortcuts.
* **Widget Configurations & Styles:** Your custom layouts, shapes, colors, and shortcut settings are saved locally on your phone.
* **Local Backups:** Any backup files (`.json`) you export or import are handled locally on your device's file system or through your private Google Drive account via standard Android system backups.

---

### In-App Purchases & Pro Mode Status

IconNest offers an optional one-time purchase to unlock Pro features.

* **Google Play Billing Only:** Transactions and Pro status checks are handled locally on your device by Google Play Services using the standard `com.android.vending.BILLING` permission.
* **No Remote Verification:** IconNest does not connect to any external servers to check purchases or licenses.
* **No Payment Data:** We do not receive, view, or store payment information (such as credit card details or billing addresses). Purchases are handled entirely by Google under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

---

### Device Permissions

* **In-App Billing (`com.android.vending.BILLING`):** Allows your device's Google Play Store app to process the optional one-time Pro upgrade and check license status locally.
* **Run at Startup (`RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`):** Allows your device to re-initialize your custom widgets on your home screen after a restart.
* **Vibration (`VIBRATE`):** Provides haptic feedback while customizing widgets.

---

### Analytics, Tracking, & Third Parties

* **No Analytics or Tracking:** IconNest contains no analytics SDKs, ad networks, tracking tools, or remote crash reporting services.
* **No Third-Party Sharing:** Because no data is collected, nothing is ever sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

---

### Children's Privacy

IconNest is suitable for general audiences. IconNest does not collect any personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
